What is PocketCrew?

Practice money skills.
One family payday at a time.

PocketCrew is a parent-managed household work and money-learning app for families with children ages 6–18. It tracks what children do, what parents approve, and how approved earnings are divided. It is a ledger—not a bank account or a payment service.

  1. Choose the work

    Parents assign responsibilities: expected contributions that normally do not pay. They also offer optional paid gigs with an agreed reward. Editable gig presets start at $1–$2.50.

  2. Finish & review

    Children start or claim work, complete the steps, and submit a note or proof. Parents approve it, request a redo, or reject it with a reason. Children cannot approve their own work.

  3. Make it payday

    An approved gig becomes pending earnings, not spendable money. A parent runs payday to post those earnings to the child's jars. An unpaid responsibility never earns money.

  4. Split & grow

    Spend tracks purchase money, Save supports savings goals, and Give tracks generosity. Each child can have a different split totaling 100%.

A simple exampleA $2 gig with a 50% Spend / 40% Save / 10% Give split adds $1 to Spend, 80¢ to Save, and 20¢ to Give when the parent runs payday. These are recorded family balances, not a bank transfer.

For parents & guardians

  • Create child profiles, assign work, and review submissions.
  • Set each child's jar percentages and the family payday schedule.
  • Track goals, record purchases or gifts, and review transaction history.
  • Invite a co-parent to the same cloud family and share updates across signed-in devices.

For children & teens

  • See their own work, claim eligible gigs, and submit completed tasks.
  • Follow approvals, upcoming payday, jar balances, and savings goals.
  • Browse shopping results, save wishes, and ask a parent to approve a purchase.
  • Use their profile passcode; no child email address is required.

Shopping is a request, not checkout. The shop uses Google Shopping results through SerpApi. Prices are snapshots and can differ by seller, variant, tax, and shipping. Parent approval records a Spend deduction; it does not place an order or charge a card. Parents make real purchases separately. A wishlist gift can be recorded without deducting the child's balance.

This is a developer preview. The Mac app’s cloud access uses a guardian account; child views use device-local profile passcodes, not separate child cloud accounts. Its shopping results should be supervised. The Mac download is not notarized. The phone web app supports local work, reviews, payday, jars, goals and history; cloud sign-in, syncing and shopping are not connected there yet.

A few things to know.

A little guidance before you get your crew together.

How do I install the Mac app?
  1. Download and open the DMG.
  2. Drag PocketCrew into the Applications folder.
  3. Open PocketCrew from Applications. Choose the demo or sign in with your existing account.

This preview is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks it, that is a distribution limitation of this build. Don’t disable your Mac’s security protections.

How do I put PocketCrew on my iPhone?

Open the phone app in Safari and tap “Put on Home Screen.” Use Safari’s Share menu, choose Add to Home Screen, then confirm Add. Open the new icon before creating a family. No ZIP, Xcode or Apple Developer membership is needed.

How do I use the same family on another device?

Use the same guardian account, or accept a co-parent invitation with the invited email. Don’t create a second family. Cloud sync needs an internet connection; the demo is local to each device. Profile passcodes are set up separately on each device.

Does PocketCrew move real money?

No. PocketCrew records family decisions and simulated balances. Parents handle real purchases, gifts, and payments outside the app. No funds are held, insured, or transferred by PocketCrew.
